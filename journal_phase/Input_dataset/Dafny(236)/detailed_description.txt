This Dafny code implements a binary search tree (BST) along with methods for inserting nodes, verifying BST properties, and printing the numbers in an in-order traversal. The `BuildBST` method constructs a BST from a sequence of integers, ensuring that there are no duplicates and that the resulting tree maintains the properties of a BST. Additional methods and predicates are provided to validate the tree structure, check for ascending order, handle insertion, and demonstrate proof obligations around the properties of the BST during the insertion process.
