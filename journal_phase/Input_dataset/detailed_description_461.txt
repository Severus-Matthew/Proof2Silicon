The Dafny code defines a `CarPark` class that models a parking facility with functionalities for regular and reserved spaces, including subscriptions for special access. It includes methods for entering and leaving the parking lots, checking availability, managing subscriptions, and enforcing various constraints like ensuring that capacities are not exceeded and maintaining the validity of the parking state at all times. Additionally, the `Main` and `MainB` methods contain assertions to verify that the functionalities work as expected, testing different scenarios such as car entry, subscription management, and park closure.
